## ⏱️ Your 20 minutes

| Time | Step | The big idea |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 → 3:00 | 🚩 **1. Offside, properly** | The rule that almost everybody gets slightly wrong |
| 3:00 → 6:00 | 🧩 **2. Shape & Formations** | Why 4-3-3 is not just three numbers |
| 6:00 → 9:00 | ⚡ **3. Pressing** | Winning the ball back — how, where and *when* |
| 9:00 → 12:00 | 📊 **4. Expected Goals (xG)** | The number that tells you who *deserved* to win |

After each step there is a **⚡ Quick check**. Try it *before* you read the answer
notes. Your stars and scores are saved in this browser.

## 🧠 How to use it

1. Press **Start** on the timer.
2. Read the step. Look at the picture — it is part of the lesson.
3. Do the quick check. Wrong answers explain themselves.
4. At 14:00, go to **The Final Test**. No scrolling back for answers — manager rules. 😄

> ## 🎯 The goal
> After 20 minutes you can explain offside to your dad, name a formation by looking
> at it, say *why* a team is pressing, and prove that possession is not the same as
> winning.
