# 🧩 Step 2: Shape & Formations
**⏱️ 3:00 → 6:00 (3 minutes)**

## 🎯 Goal

Read a formation **and** know what it is *for*.

---

## 🧠 How to read the numbers

A formation lists the outfield players **from defence to attack** (the goalkeeper is
always the extra 1):

| Formation | Defence | Midfield | Attack | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **4-4-2** | 4 | 4 | 2 | Two flat banks of four. Solid. Easy to defend. |
| **4-3-3** | 4 | 3 | 3 | Wide forwards stretch the pitch. Midfield triangle. |
| **3-5-2** | 3 | 5 | 2 | Wing-backs run the whole side. Strong middle. |
| **4-2-3-1** | 4 | 2 + 3 | 1 | Two holders, one creative 10 behind a lone striker. |

## 🔍 The shape CHANGES when you attack and defend

Real teams don't stay in one picture:

- **Back four → back three.** A full-back "inverts" into midfield, so in possession the team looks like a **3-2-5** or similar.
- **A 3-5-2 without the ball** often becomes a **5-3-2**: wing-backs drop back to form a *back five*.
- **The "false nine":** a striker who drops *deep* into midfield. Centre-backs must decide — follow him (and leave a hole) or let him go (and he has time).

## 🧠 The key tradeoff: width vs. compactness

| If the team is... | Good | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Wide** | Stretches defenders, opens gaps | Big spaces between players |
| **Compact** (narrow & close) | Hard to pass through | Can be beaten on the wings |

🔑 **A manager's job:** pick the shape that uses *your* best players and attacks the *other
team's* weakness. A formation has **no** magic on its own.

> ## 🎵 Say this:
> ## Numbers describe where players **start**. Tactics describe where they **go**.
