# 📖 Football Words (Glossary)
**Reference**

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Offside position** | Closer to the goal line than the ball and the second-last opponent |
| **Back four / back three** | The number of defenders in a line |
| **Wing-back** | A wide player who defends *and* attacks the whole side |
| **False nine** | A striker who drops deep into midfield |
| **Inverted full-back** | A full-back who steps into midfield when attacking |
| **Compact** | Players close together — little space between lines |
| **High line** | The defenders stand far up the pitch, near halfway |
| **Low block** | Defending deep, close to your own goal |
| **Press / pressing** | Winning the ball back by closing down quickly |
| **Counter-press (gegenpress)** | Pressing immediately after you lose the ball |
| **Trigger** | The signal that starts a press |
| **PPDA** | Passes Per Defensive Action — lower = more intense press |
| **xG** | Expected goals — the chance a shot becomes a goal |
| **Transition** | The moment the ball changes team — attack ↔ defence |
| **Clean sheet** | Not conceding a goal |
| **Stoppage time** | Extra minutes at the end of each half |
