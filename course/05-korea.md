## 🕰️ The timeline

| Year | Moment | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **1970s–80s** | **Cha Bum-kun** becomes a star in the German **Bundesliga** | Proof that a Korean player could be among the best in Europe |
| **2002** | Korea co-hosts the **World Cup** and reaches the **semi-final** (finishes 4th) | Beat **Italy** and **Spain** on the way — still the best Asian World Cup run |
| **2005–2012** | **Park Ji-sung** at **Manchester United** | A midfielder famous for **running and pressing** — the work-rate you learned in Step 3 |
| **2018** | Korea beat **Germany 2-0** in the group stage | The reigning champions went **out**. Son Heung-min scored in stoppage time |
| **2021–22** | **Son Heung-min** shares the Premier League **Golden Boot** — 23 goals, **no penalties** | None from the penalty spot — every goal came from a chance he had to create and finish |
| **2022** | Korea **beat Portugal 2-1** with a stoppage-time winner by **Hwang Hee-chan** | Qualified for the last 16 |

## 🧠 Connect it to the course

- **Counter-attack from a low block:** In 2018 Korea **defended compactly**, and Germany pushed **everyone** forward — even their goalkeeper. The second goal went into an **empty net**.
- **Offside:** a high line **invites** that long pass — the *timing* of the run and the line is everything.
- **VAR & offside:** Korea's first goal in that match was first flagged **offside** by the assistant, then **given** after a VAR review. Exactly the 'second-last opponent' line from Step 1!
- **xG:** one match proves little — but **Son's** consistency over many seasons is what xG people look at.
