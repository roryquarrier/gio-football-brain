# ⚡ Step 3: Pressing
**⏱️ 6:00 → 9:00 (3 minutes)**

## 🎯 Goal

Understand pressing as a **plan**, not just "running a lot".

---

## 🧠 Three ways to defend

| Style | Where you defend | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| **High press** | In the *opponent's* half | Win the ball close to their goal → short route to a shot |
| **Mid block** | Around the halfway line | Stay compact, pounce on mistakes |
| **Low block** | Near your own box | Give up space, protect the goal, counter-attack |

## 🎯 Pressing triggers

A good team doesn't press all the time. It presses on a **trigger** — a signal:

- a **backward pass** to the goalkeeper or centre-back,
- a **bad touch** (the ball bounces away from the receiver's feet),
- the ball goes **wide** to the touchline (the sideline is an extra "defender"),
- the receiver has his **back to the play**.

## 🔄 Counter-press (gegenpress)

> Lose the ball → **immediately** try to win it back, while the opponent is still
> disorganised — not run home first.

The idea is the **moments after losing possession** are the best time to attack:
the other team's shape is *broken* and they are close to your goal.

## 📏 A stat for it: PPDA

**PPDA = Passes Per Defensive Action** — how many passes the opponent is allowed to
make *before* you tackle, intercept or foul. **Lower = more aggressive press.**
10 means they get ~10 passes; 20 means you're sitting back.

## ⚠️ The cost

Pressing **costs energy** and **leaves space behind** the defence. If the press is
beaten with one long pass, you're in trouble.
Which is why a 90-minute pressing team needs **fitness, courage and good timing**.

> ## 🎵 Say this:
> ## A press is a **trap**, not a sprint.
