# 🚩 Step 1: Offside, Properly
**⏱️ 0:00 → 3:00 (3 minutes)**

## 🎯 Goal

Say the offside rule **exactly** — the way a referee must apply it.

---

## 🧠 The rule in one sentence

> ## A player is in an **offside position** if any part of their **head, body or feet**
> ## is **closer to the opponents' goal line** than **both** the ball **and** the **second-last opponent**.

Notice four things most fans forget:

| Detail | What it means |
|---|---|
| **"Second-last opponent"** | Usually that is the *last defender* — because the goalkeeper is the last opponent. But if the keeper runs up the pitch, a **defender can be the "last" player and the line moves.** |
| **Level = onside** | If you are *exactly level* with the second-last defender, you are **onside**. |
| **Arms don't count** | Hands and arms are **not** used to judge it. A shoulder is — a hand is not. |
| **Own half = onside** | You can **never** be offside in your own half. |

## ⚠️ Being in an offside position is NOT a foul!

Stand there all day — nothing happens. The **offence** only happens when:

1. a teammate **plays or touches the ball**, and
2. you then become **involved in active play** — you play the ball, **interfere with an opponent**, or **gain an advantage** from the position.

So an attacker can be in an offside position and the referee waves play on, because he
didn't touch it and didn't stop the defender seeing it. Referees judge **involvement**.

## 🔒 Three times you can NOT be offside

You **cannot** be penalised for offside **directly** from:

- a **goal kick**
- a **throw-in**
- a **corner kick**

## 🔑 The sneaky one: *deliberate play*

If a defender **deliberately plays** the ball (a real pass, clearance or controlled touch),
the attacker is **no longer offside** — a new moment starts. A **deflection** or a
desperate block does **not** reset it. The referee must decide: *did he **play** it, or just
get in the way of it?*

💡 **Technology:** at big tournaments, semi-automated systems track the players' limbs
and the ball to help VAR draw the line quickly. But the *rule* is still the same one you
just learned.

> ## 🎵 Say this:
> ## Position is not an offence. **Involvement** is.
