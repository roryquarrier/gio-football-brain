# 🧑‍🏫 For Grown-Ups
**Reference**

**What this is:** a 20-minute, self-paced mini-course for an ~11-year-old, strong
reader, football-obsessed student from South Korea. English is the language of
instruction; football vocabulary is introduced in context and collected in the glossary.

**Structure:** four idea steps (3 min each) → a short Korea story (2 min) → a 12-question
final test (6 min). Each idea step ends with a quick-check quiz. The test mixes
**image-based** and **text** questions, and some need two ideas combined.

**Why football analysis?** It uses the child's real interest to practise **logic,
probability** (xG), **spatial reasoning** (formations, offside) and **explaining
reasons** in English — all in a context he already cares about.

**Progress:** saved in the browser (`localStorage`) on this device only. No accounts,
no tracking. The student's name and progress can be exported/imported as a file.

**Images:** illustrations were generated with OpenAI image models and contain no real
people, logos or text.

**Facts:** historical items (2002 semi-final, 2018 win over Germany, 2022 win over
Portugal, Son Heung-min's 2021–22 Golden Boot) are well documented; the xG values are
**approximate averages** and differ slightly between providers.
